Movement

Body motion

Connect a robot to use its motions.

Walk, Run and Crawl

Combine travel and turning while moving. Hold two direction keys or use both controller axes to follow a curve. Direction presets replace both values.

0–100% walks. Above 100% transitions to paired-leg Run; 200% is full Run.

Walk, Run, Crawl and Crab use fast coordinated strokes. Speed selects stride distance; shorter strokes finish sooner. The reindexed-crank Walk profile uses about 62 mm at full stride and 31 mm at 50%. Its body rises and falls through the stride. Above 100%, the Walk control transitions into bounding Run, reaching full Run stride at 200%. Direct stride selects 1–100% of the chosen gait’s range. These controls set movement distance, not motor power. Changes apply while moving; Finish settles the feet. Finish before selecting a different gait. Direction changes while moving are available when the steering controls are shown.

Connection and power profile

0 disables automatic rest. Active movement and speech defer rest. The robot lays down normally and keeps its servos powered; new commands remain available. Saved across power cycles.

Self-leveling

Uses IMU tilt to adjust Stand and walking. Off smoothly removes the correction while the current motion continues. Saved on the robot across restarts.

1× keeps the original response. Higher strength corrects tilt more aggressively; higher damping resists rocking. 0° targets the normal body angle. Save on robot applies all fields together. These controls do not change sensor calibration or motor torque.

Level reference and gyro calibration

Place the body at the angle you want it to hold. This copies the measured roll and pitch into the target fields above; Save on robot applies them. It does not change the sensor axes or gyro bias.

Keep the whole robot completely still, including its legs. Any resting angle is fine. This measures 256 sensor samples, saves the gyro bias and applies it without a restart. Movement during sampling contaminates the result; repeat while still. Calibration does not switch self-leveling or move the robot.

Motion speeds

One maximum commanded joint speed for walking, named motions and entry transitions. Motions slow together to preserve their path and stride. If Run cannot reach its requested cadence, this limit is controlling its timing. Enter any positive value; there is no fixed maximum. Saving stops motion; start it again to resume.

Read from robot to load its limit.

2× plays a motion in half the time. Changes apply to the next named motion, including its entry pose. Walking uses its own Speed control. Saving stops the robot; start a motion to resume.

Camera settings

Exposure, gain and JPEG quality

Gain is amplification, not calibrated ISO. Manual gain starts at 1×. Longer exposure collects more light but can blur motion and reduce delivered FPS. Auto allows longer exposure in dim light. Higher JPEG quality uses more bandwidth; images exceeding 256 KiB report a camera error. These settings apply to preview and snapshots and are saved with this robot's camera settings.

Auto snapshot resolution uses the full view at 960p in dim light, or a cropped 1080p view in brighter light. Photos wait for exposure to settle and may take several seconds.

Metric ground-plane calibration

Measure the checkerboard square size. Capture at least three distinct tilted views at the same resolution and crop. Fit intrinsics, then place the board flat on the actual floor and set the reference. Displacement follows retained floor points; this is not depth or general visual-inertial navigation. Unknown metric uncertainty is reported as unknown.

    Servo position feedback
    
  
    Connect a P4 with servo feedback firmware.
    
  
    The fourth servo wire measures shaft position. An ADC reading alone does not prove a wire is connected or a foot is touching the floor.
    
  
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    
  
    Enable reading after wiring. Calibrate all twelve joints before enabling measured Stand. Stand keeps its fast movement commands, then holds each lift/place target until the joints arrive. A missing reading or a joint that cannot reach its target leaves that stage active; another motion command can replace it. This does not increase motor torque.
    
  
    
  
    
    Measured shaft position versus commanded position
    
    JointMUX inputADC / voltageCommand µsMeasured equivalent µsError µsReading
    
    
  
    
  
    Calibrate one feedback wire
    
  
    Use Joint calibration to move the unloaded servo to three known positions. Confirm that its shaft has arrived, then capture each position here. Low, middle and high refer to increasing command pulses; the feedback voltage may increase or decrease. Capturing never moves a servo or changes its Home settings.
    
  
    Choose Joint calibration in the sidebar to position a servo, then return here to capture its reading.
    
  
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
  
    
  
    All multiplexer inputs and wiring
    
    CD74HC4067: VCC → 3V3, GND and EN → common GND, SIG/COM → GPIO49. S0/S1/S2/S3 → GPIO27/28/29/30. Feedback voltages must remain within the module's actual supply voltage; check the full range before connecting. Keep the reader powered while the servos drive their feedback wires.
